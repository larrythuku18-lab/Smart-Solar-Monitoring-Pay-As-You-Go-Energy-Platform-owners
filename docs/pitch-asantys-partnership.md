# SolGrid × Asantys — Partnership Proposal

> **The proposition:** Asantys has spent 15 years building the best solar hardware supply chain in Africa. SolGrid has built the PAYG billing + monitoring platform that turns that hardware into revenue-generating energy businesses. **Neither company can build the other's half.** This is a proposal to combine them — as equal partners — into a single, market-leading offering for off-grid and mini-grid energy.

---

## 1 · Why this is a partnership, not a vendor deal

A partnership is only real when each side brings something the other **cannot** buy or build quickly. That's exactly the situation here:

| | What we bring | What you'd have to do without us |
|---|---|---|
| **Asantys** | 15 years of EPC delivery, tier-one supply chain (SMA, Hoppecke, UZ), Nairobi warehouse, field teams, installer network, tender relationships | *(nothing — this is your strength)* |
| **SolGrid** | Working PAYG platform: M-Pesa billing, relay enforcement, telemetry, AI, dashboards — 168 tests, shipping today | Build a fintech-grade billing + IoT platform from scratch: 2–3 years and a completely different company |

**The asymmetry is the partnership.** Your customers already need a billing/monitoring layer — they're buying it (badly) from elsewhere or building it themselves. The choice isn't "hire SolGrid" — it's whether that layer is *yours* (bundled, co-branded, revenue-shared) or someone else's.

## 2 · The joint offering

**One product, two companies:**

> **Asantys Solar-as-a-Service** — turnkey containerized solar + storage + SolGrid's PAYG brain: customers top up via M-Pesa, power flows, and the operator watches every watt and every shilling from one dashboard.

The hardware stays Asantys. The software stays SolGrid. The customer buys **one** solution from **one** partner — and the revenue is split by agreement.

### What the platform delivers

- **M-Pesa STK Push top-ups** → wallet credit → relay unlocks in seconds; zero balance cuts power automatically. (Simulation mode today, sandbox → production path ready for Safaricom KYC.)
- **Real-time telemetry** — generation, consumption, battery state, voltage/current every 30 s from ESP32-based relay units.
- **AI engine (4 models)** — 6-hour energy forecast, predictive maintenance, payment-fraud detection (auto-locks relay), load-shift optimization.
- **Dashboards** — live KPIs, analytics, deep-dive analysis board, customer portal; multi-tenant so each operator sees only its own fleet.
- **Production-grade OTA** — signed firmware updates with staged rollout and boot-failure auto-rollback; no site visits.
- **Hardened under the hood** — PostgreSQL persistence, idempotent migrations, per-device keys, observability (Prometheus metrics + alert rules), SMS/email alerts, 168 automated tests.

## 4 · How the partnership is structured (proposal)

Three layers, negotiated once, scalable from day one:

1. **Joint offering / co-sell** — Asantys proposals for mini-grid and rural-electrification tenders include the SolGrid layer as standard; revenue shared per agreed split on every bundled deal.
2. **Co-branding** — "Powered by SolGrid" on hardware, Asantys's name on every dashboard. Each company keeps its brand and its customers.
3. **Co-development roadmap** — SolGrid prioritizes integrations Asantys's fleet actually needs (SMA/BMS protocols, specific battery banks), so the joint offering gets better the longer we work together.

**Suggested first step — the shared-risk pilot (60–90 days, one site):** SolGrid stands up a live tenant on one Asantys-delivered site at our own cost; Asantys provides the site and system access. Success = a live dashboard showing collections and remote visibility that Asantys can take to its next tender. **Both companies invest in it; both companies win from it.**
